✏️ Your cards live in the Sheet

Every card is a row in the cards tab. Edit one there and this app picks it up on the next refresh — there is no second copy.

Open the cards tab ↗

There is no importer in this app — the Sheet has its own, and that import is the load:

  1. Open the cards tab above, then File → Import → Upload your CSV.
  2. Import location: Append to current sheet adds to your deck (it appends to the cards tab you just landed on). Replace current sheet is a fresh start — it erases existing cards and progress.
  3. Set “Convert text to numbers, dates, and formulas” → No. This matters: otherwise Google mangles entries like 1/2 into dates.

Importing is a laptop/desktop task — the Sheets mobile app can’t do File → Import.

✨ A paragraph from your mistakes

Drilling shows a word on its own. This collects the ones you keep getting wrong and asks for a short paragraph that uses all of them, so you meet them in a sentence instead.

Paste it into whatever AI you use and read the paragraph there — nothing comes back into the app, and no card is touched.

🎧 Where sentences live

Soak plays whole sentences aloud, one at a time, with a silence after each — no front, no back, nothing to grade. They live in their own sentences tab of the same Sheet:

  1. Tap Open the sentences tab below, then add a row.
  2. text — the sentence itself. This is the only column that gets spoken, so it must be in the language you're studying.
  3. note — your translation, or the thing to notice (a tense, a clause order). Shown on screen, never read aloud.
  4. tag — optional label like perfectum or bijzin, shown as a badge. Leave id, added and exclude blank.

Keep the app in front while it plays — phones suspend speech when the screen locks or you switch apps, so Soak pauses itself and waits for you.
